Getting started

Recommended order for a new Manager:

  1. Add your inventories (stockrooms or shelves) under All inventories → Add inventory.
  2. Create categories, then products, from the Catalogue; link each product to the inventories that stock it.
  3. Set each product's lead time and safety stock (Reorder settings) and, if you want values, its selling price per inventory.
  4. Invite your Staff under Team → Staff invitations and choose the inventories they work in.
  5. Record opening stock with Receive stock or a CSV import, then verify demand each day.

The Dashboard link and the Tory logo always bring you back to the dashboard.

Inventories Manager

Everyone sees only the inventories they are assigned to; pick one with the Current inventory switcher. Its pages (Products, Insights, and for Managers Import CSV and Verify demand) are grouped under its name in the sidebar.

Managers add inventories and edit the name, icon, colour and optional picture. Choose who can use each inventory under Who can use this inventory on its Edit page. To deactivate one, open Edit and type its name to confirm; reactivate it from All inventories. Names are unique within the business, ignoring case. Every change is recorded in the activity log.

Products and categories Manager Staff view

  • Each product has a name, a SKU, a base unit (for example box), whether it tracks expiry dates, an optional category, and lead time and safety stock for reordering.
  • A SKU is unique within the business and cannot be changed or reused once created.
  • Selling prices are set per inventory and are used only for retail stock value.
  • A product can be deactivated only when its physical stock is zero in every inventory (quarantined units count) and you are assigned to every inventory it is linked to. Inactive products can't be received or issued.
  • Categories are per business; a category in use by a product can't be deleted. The catalogue and product lists can be filtered by category.

Receiving stock Manager Staff

Open a product and choose Receive stock. Enter a whole number of base units. Products that track expiry need an expiry date, which can't be in the past. Each receipt becomes a numbered batch and appears in the product's stock history.

Issuing stock (FEFO and FIFO) Manager Staff

Enter how many units left the shelf; Tory chooses the batches:

  1. Dated batches first, earliest expiry first (FEFO).
  2. Then undated batches in the order they were received (FIFO).

Quarantined units are never issued, and you can't issue more than is available. Issues are the demand that forecasts use.

Count adjustments and disposal Manager

Adjust count: enter the counted quantity for each batch you checked, confirm each batch, and give a reason. Tory records the difference. Adjustments are not demand, so they don't change forecasts.

Dispose stock: remove quarantined units with a reason code (Expired, Damaged or Other; Other needs a note). Disposals are recorded and are not demand.

Expiry and quarantine

At 00:00 Singapore time on a batch's expiry date its remaining units become quarantined: still physically counted, but no longer available to issue. A scheduled job records the quarantine (shown as System in history). A Manager then disposes of the units. The dashboard lists stock expiring in the next 30 days.

Verifying demand Manager

Forecasts use only demand a Manager has confirmed. After a Singapore day ends, check that every issue for that day was recorded, then verify it, either per product on the forecast page or for a whole inventory with Verify demand. You can verify the last seven complete days.

If an issue is later recorded for a day you already verified, that day becomes invalidated and must be verified again. You can also mark a verified day incomplete, with a reason.

Forecasts and reorder suggestions Manager Staff

A forecast is available only when all of the previous seven complete days are verified. Unverified days are never treated as zero.

  • Average daily demand = units issued over those 7 days ÷ 7
  • Reorder point = average daily demand × lead time (days) + safety stock
  • Reorder alert when available units ≤ reorder point
  • Suggested order = average daily demand × (14 + lead time) + safety stock − available, rounded up (never below 0). It covers 14 days of demand after the order arrives.

Worked example. 21 units were issued over the last 7 verified days, so the average is 3 a day. With a lead time of 4 days and safety stock of 5, the reorder point is 3 × 4 + 5 = 17. With 10 units available, 10 ≤ 17 raises an alert, and the suggested order is 3 × (14 + 4) + 5 − 10 = 49 units.

Only Managers can change lead time and safety stock. They apply to the product in every inventory.

Insights and dashboard metrics Manager Staff

Out of stock
Active products in the inventory with 0 available units.
Low stock
Available units at or below the reorder point.
Days of supply
Available units ÷ verified average daily demand. Shown as "—" with a reason when there is no forecast or demand is zero.
Stock-to-sales (30 days)
Current on-hand units ÷ units issued over the last 30 complete Singapore days.
Turnover (30 days)
Units issued over the last 30 complete days ÷ average day-end on-hand units over those days, rebuilt from the movement history.
Retail stock value
Available units × the inventory's selling price, in SGD. Products without a price are left out and counted separately.
Unverified demand days
Product-days in the review window that still need verification.

The dashboard covers all your inventories (or one, using the filter) and lists what needs action first. Insights charts each explain what they show and how to read them, and each has a data table.

CSV import and export

Manager Import CSV applies every row to the inventory you selected. The file has the header sku,change. A positive change receives undated stock (only for products that don't track expiry), and a negative change issues stock. Download the template from the import page. Importing the exact same file into the same inventory twice is rejected.

Manager Staff Download current stock per inventory and a product's full history. Manager Download the activity log. In every export, cells starting with = + - or @ are prefixed with an apostrophe so spreadsheets don't run them as formulas.

Staff and roles Manager

  • Managers manage inventories, products, categories, prices, reorder settings, imports, counts, disposals, demand verification, Staff and the activity log, within the inventories they are assigned to.
  • Staff receive and issue stock, and view products, history, forecasts, insights and the dashboard, in their assigned inventories only.
  • Invitations create a new Staff account through a single-use link that expires after 24 hours. You can revoke or resend it, and send up to 5 a day. Choose at least one of your inventories.
  • Deactivating a Staff account signs it out everywhere on its next request and keeps its history.

Activity log Manager Staff

One read-only list of who did what across your business: receipts and issues (including CSV rows), count adjustments, disposals, quarantines, demand reviews, product and inventory changes, inventory access changes, and invitations sent. Filter by user, action, inventory, product or dates. Managers can download the filtered list as CSV.

Staff see stock movements (received, issued, counted and quarantined, including CSV imports) in the inventories they are assigned to. Settings and access changes, other inventories and the CSV download are for Managers.

Profile and password Manager Staff

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. You can set a display name (shown instead of your email), job title, phone, avatar colour and an optional photo. Photos are resized and their location data is removed.

Passwords need at least 15 characters. Changing yours signs out your other sessions. Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity or 8 hours in total. A password reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes.
