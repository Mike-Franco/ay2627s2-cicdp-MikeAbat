# Tory Requirements gap and consistency analysis

**Status:** QA analysis updated for owner-provided G-01/G-02/G-09 Option A approvals dated 1 November 2026. BR-001–BR-045 and the six recorded nonfunctional decisions remain individually approved; this analysis does not approve the complete SRS or stage exit. The SRS and Requirements exit remain **PENDING OWNER APPROVAL**. Design has not been authorised.

## Executive summary

The six Requirements records consistently retain the 45 owner-approved BR IDs, 12 FR IDs and 11 NFR IDs. Later BRs refine earlier wording: BR-038 makes SKU reservation permanent, BR-030 changes the reorder stock input to available-to-issue, BR-034 orders dated before undated batches, BR-035 fixes the Singapore expiry boundary, BR-039 fixes CSV inventory selection, BR-040 fixes the all-inventory zero-stock guard, and BR-041 fixes exact-byte, inventory-scoped duplicate identity. These are refinements, not competing approvals.

**Nineteen open gaps: 9 blocking, 8 conditional, 2 nonblocking; G-01 requirements behaviour resolved with Design verification still open.** “Blocking” means the SRS cannot be accepted as deterministic without an owner decision or an explicit, bounded deferral that states the required outcome and target stage. “Conditional” needs an owner-recorded Design-stage disposition and feasibility check. “Nonblocking” can remain proposed without weakening the baseline. G-02 still lacks a maximum evidence-recording delay; G-09 still lacks a decision on separate rejected-attempt logging. The verification column describes future evidence, not completed tests.

No direct contradiction among the approved BRs was found. The material cross-record tension is historical wording: the Planning charter, P1, feasibility assessment, owner decisions and ADR-001 describe CI provider as undecided, while the later 1 November Requirements decision selects GitHub Actions for PR Django/PostgreSQL tests. Those Planning records remain valid as dated decisions; the later decision governs, and their historical candidate language must not be read as the current status. SRS §2.2 also excludes a “detailed pipeline choice” while NFR-010 names GitHub Actions; clarify that this exclusion means workflow design, not the already approved CI service. The exact `sku,change` header remains approved; no batch/expiry CSV columns are inferred.

**Evidence reviewed:** all six files in this directory; [Charter](../planning/PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [Schedule](../planning/PROJECT_SCHEDULE.md), [Feasibility](../planning/FEASIBILITY_ASSESSMENT.md), [Risk Register](../planning/RISK_REGISTER.md), [P1 Gate](../planning/PLANNING_GATE_P1.md), [Planning Review](../planning/PLANNING_REVIEW.md), [Planning Owner Decisions](../planning/OWNER_DECISIONS.md), and [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001-backend-and-database.md). The isolated PoC and its reported test counts are not Tory acceptance evidence.
