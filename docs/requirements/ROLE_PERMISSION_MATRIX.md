**Status:** BR-021–BR-025 approve the listed role actions and assigned-inventory scope; BR-003, BR-026–BR-028, BR-037 and BR-042–BR-045 add specific account and security constraints. **TORY-11 owner decision, 16 November 2026:** initial Manager provisioning is operator-run. The bootstrap command creates a new business and initial inventories and explicitly assigns the ordinary Manager to each created inventory. This does not expand Manager permissions or resolve other account administration.

**TORY-11 Staff invitation owner decisions, 16 November 2026:** invitations create new Staff accounts only; each requires at least one currently Manager-authorised inventory in the same business, checked at issuance and redemption. These decisions do not settle general multi-business account policy.

**TORY-11 Staff deactivation owner decision, 18 November 2026:** an active Manager may deactivate an ordinary Staff account only if its sole business membership is in that Manager's business and every assignment falls within the Manager's current authorized inventory scope. All membership and assignment rows count, including inactive rows. Manager, Django-admin/superuser and self targets are denied. This is a restricted whole-account action; Staff reactivation and general multi-business account policy remain unresolved.

## Proposed authorization principles

- Authenticate Manager and Staff with Django-based email/password sign-in; use server-managed sessions, HttpOnly cookies, HTTPS and CSRF protection (BR-042/BR-045).
- Enforce permissions server-side on every operation; hiding a control is not authorization. Authentication never grants inventory access beyond BR-022/BR-027.
- Deny unlisted actions by default (**PROPOSED**).
- Enforce Manager assignment scope against the Manager's current authorization and business boundary (BR-027); the concurrency policy remains TBD.
- Apply BR-021–BR-025 approved cells and resolve only unlisted role/action cells and mechanics before Design.
- Do not treat an owner-defined role name as evidence of exact permissions or stakeholder interviews.
