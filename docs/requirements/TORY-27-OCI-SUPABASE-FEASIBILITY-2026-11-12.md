**Budget:** SGD 0. The owner permits a card for hosting account verification only; paid plans, usage and unguarded charges remain prohibited. This note supplements [the deployment feasibility and owner decision record](DEPLOYMENT_FEASIBILITY_GAP_OWNER_DECISIONS.md).

## Assessment

## Finding and gates

Neither OCI nor Supabase is proven to meet the complete TORY-27 staging requirements at SGD 0. OCI provides self-managed control but eligibility/capacity, paid-resource safeguards and reliable operations remain unproven. Supabase documents a potential path for the commit-timestamp setting, but Free lacks automatic backups, may pause, and does not host Django or its five-minute command. Supabase is assessed as a possible database component only. **No provider is recommended or selected.**

Before considering a provider, collect evidence for: (1) eligible account, region/capacity, exact quotas and safeguards that prevent unapproved charges; (2) actual PostgreSQL version, active commit timestamp setting, required Tory functions and migrations; (3) Django 5.2 hosting with HTTPS, secure secrets, static assets, migrations and repeatable Manager provisioning; (4) five-minute expiry execution, monitoring and observed recovery within one hour; (5) encrypted, access-controlled daily off-host backups with retention, failure alerting and a successful isolated restore; and (6) email sender, delivery and cost controls. Provider marketing claims or public plan limits alone are insufficient.

The owner decision permits card use for account verification only; it does not approve paid usage, a provider, or a budget change. TORY-27 and TORY-19 remain **incomplete** until their acceptance evidence exists. This assessment claims no provider account, deployment, backup, restore or staging test.
