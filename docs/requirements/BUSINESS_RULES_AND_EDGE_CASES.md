## Edge-case acceptance examples to approve

The following deterministic examples apply approved rules; they are proposed acceptance cases, not executed tests. Assume every listed day has independently verified complete observation coverage.

| Case | Input | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Verified zero and two-decimal display | For 1–7 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore), daily stock-out demand is `[1, 0, 1, 0, 1, 0, 0]`; zero days have verified coverage. | Total `3`; unrounded average `3/7`; displayed average `0.43`. The zero days count as zero (BR-007–BR-009). |
| Missing coverage | Same series, but 3 November lacks reliable observation coverage, even if no stock-out record exists. | Forecast and dependent recommendation are unavailable; do not substitute zero (BR-008). |
| Singapore midnight | At 8 November 2026 00:00 Asia/Singapore, window is 1 November 00:00 through 7 November 23:59:59… local time. | A stock-out at 7 November 23:59 is included; at 8 November 00:00 is excluded; at 31 October 23:59 is excluded (BR-007). |
| No intermediate rounding | For the verified `3/7` average above, lead time `7` days, valid safety stock `0` and available-to-issue quantity `0`. | Reorder point `(3/7) × 7 + 0 = 3`; suggested quantity `3`. Using displayed `0.43` would incorrectly yield `ceil(3.01) = 4` (BR-009–BR-011, BR-015, BR-030). |
| Alert at threshold | Reorder point `8`; available-to-issue `8`; pending delivery `5`. | Show reorder alert and suggested quantity `0` separately; pending delivery does not change the calculation (BR-012/BR-013). |
| Quarantine and reorder input | On-hand `10`, of which `4` are quarantined; reorder point `8`; assume no other holds. | Available-to-issue quantity `6`; suggested quantity `2` (BR-030). Quarantine/disposal movements contribute zero forecast demand (BR-031). |
| FEFO and eligibility | Two eligible batches expire on different dates; a third batch is expired and quarantined. | Ordinary issue allocates from the earlier eligible expiry first; quarantined units cannot satisfy it (BR-029/BR-033–BR-035). Equal-expiry ties remain TBD. |
| FEFO before undated FIFO | Eligible dated batches expire 10 and 12 November; undated batches were received first and second. Issue consumes more than the dated quantity. | Consume 10 November, then 12 November, then the first undated receipt, then the second (BR-033/BR-034). Equal-expiry ties remain TBD. |
| Singapore midnight expiry | A batch expires 8 November 2026. Compare ordinary issue at 7 November 23:59:59 and 8 November 00:00 Asia/Singapore. | It remains eligible before midnight, then is unavailable for ordinary issue from 00:00 on 8 November; audit/quarantine transition mechanism TBD (BR-029/BR-035). |
| Atomic CSV positive-row rejection | In one `sku,change` upload for an explicitly selected authorized inventory, a valid negative row is followed by a positive row for an expiry-tracked product. | Reject the entire file with no stock mutation or demand record; receive the expiry-tracked product manually with batch/expiry details. Header stays `sku,change` (BR-004/BR-036). |
| Quarantined disposal | Manager submits disposal of `2` quarantined units with a reason; Staff submits the same action or Manager omits the reason. | Only the reasoned Manager action may commit, with actor/history retained; Staff and missing-reason actions are denied without mutation. Committed disposal contributes no demand (BR-031/BR-037). |
| Permanently reserved SKU | Product A in business X used SKU `ABC` and was deactivated. Manager attempts to give `abc` to new product B in X. | Reject reuse for B even though A is inactive; A's original SKU remains reserved (BR-001/BR-017/BR-038). Rename/alias behavior for A remains TBD. |
| Cross-inventory deactivation guard | A product has zero ordinary stock in inventory 1 but `1` quarantined physical unit in inventory 2 of the same business. | Reject deactivation until **all** business inventories have zero physical units, including quarantine (BR-028/BR-040). How remaining stock is resolved remains TBD. |
| CSV inventory scope | Manager selects authorized inventory A before uploading two valid `sku,change` rows; inventory B has the same catalogue products. | Both committed rows affect A only; B remains unchanged. An unselected or unauthorized inventory cannot be used (BR-039). |
| Inventory-scoped byte duplicate | File bytes `F` imported successfully for inventory A. Re-upload `F` to A; separately upload `F` to authorized inventory B, assuming rows otherwise valid. | Reject re-upload to A with no mutation. B's upload is not rejected **solely** as a duplicate of A's success. A byte-different file with equivalent records is not identical under BR-041 (BR-005/BR-041). Concurrent same-inventory uploads remain TBD. |

These cases require dated stock movement evidence and a trustworthy indication that each day was observed. The observation mechanism and database schema belong to later decisions.

1. Stock-out exactly equal to available stock; one unit over available stock; repeated concurrent stock-outs.
2. Adjustment below zero, adjustment to zero, and adjustment with missing reason if reasons are approved.
3. CSV valid rows around an invalid row; unknown SKU; repeated SKU; repeated whole file; malformed quotes; empty/header-only file; UTF-8 BOM; maximum size boundary.
4. Forecast all-zero fully observed week; one missing day; no history; demand transaction at day boundary; adjustment in window; lead time zero/one; reorder quantity fractional before ceiling.
5. Product SKU case/space variations and attempts to rename/deactivate a product with movements.

No example is an executed test. The individually approved rules are recorded separately; remaining details require owner disposition before Requirements exit.
