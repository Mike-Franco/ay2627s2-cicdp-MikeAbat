## Edge-case acceptance examples

The following deterministic examples apply approved rules; they are acceptance plans, not executed tests. Assume every listed forecast day has independently verified complete observation coverage.

| Case | Input | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Required base-unit label and whole-unit receipt | Product P has an explicitly configured base-unit label; submit receipt quantities `1`, `0`, `-1`, and `1.5`. | `1` is a whole base-unit receipt; zero, negative and fractional quantities reject. Packaging conversion is deferred. Each product requires a label (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026). |
| Per-receipt and physical-balance maximum | Submit `2,147,483,647` units to an empty product/inventory; then test a receipt of `2,147,483,648`. Separately, physical stock including quarantine is `2,147,483,646` and a two-unit receipt is submitted. | Maximum receipt and balance pass; either overflow rejects atomically. Quarantined units count toward the product/inventory balance limit (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026). |
| Expiry date on receipt | At 5 November 2026 00:00 Asia/Singapore, submit expiry dates 4 November, 5 November, and 6 November. | The first two reject; the future date is eligible subject to expiry-tracking configuration (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026). |
| Explicit expiry configuration and optional manual expiry | Product P has mandatory expiry tracking; product Q does not. Attempt receipts before configuring P or Q. Then submit P without a date, Q with a future date, and Q without a date. | No product receives before its expiry configuration is explicit. P requires a future date. Q may omit expiry or supply a future date; a supplied date participates in expiry eligibility and dated FEFO, while an undated receipt follows approved undated ordering. CSV rules remain unchanged (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026; G-01/G-03). |
| Manual receiving request token | Submit token T with input X; replay T/X; replay T with changed input Y; submit X with fresh token U. | Replay T/X returns the original without another receipt or audit event, after authorization is checked. T/Y is rejected. U/X creates a distinct receipt (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026). |
| Assignment revocation and receipt race | In two PostgreSQL transactions, commit assignment revocation before the receipt locks authorization; repeat with the receipt acquiring required authorization locks first. | Revocation-first denies receipt. Receipt-lock-first may complete before revocation commits. Exact lock implementation remains Design/G-11 (TORY-15 owner decision, 5 November 2026). |
| Verified zero and two-decimal display | For 1–7 November 2026 (Asia/Singapore), daily stock-out demand is `[1, 0, 1, 0, 1, 0, 0]`; zero days have verified coverage. | Total `3`; unrounded average `3/7`; displayed average `0.43`. The zero days count as zero (BR-007–BR-009). |
| Missing coverage | Same series, but 3 November lacks reliable observation coverage, even if no stock-out record exists. | Forecast and dependent recommendation are unavailable; do not substitute zero (BR-008). |
| Singapore midnight | At 8 November 2026 00:00 Asia/Singapore, window is 1 November 00:00 through 7 November 23:59:59… local time. | A stock-out at 7 November 23:59 is included; at 8 November 00:00 is excluded; at 31 October 23:59 is excluded (BR-007). |
| No intermediate rounding | For the verified `3/7` average above, lead time `7` days, valid safety stock `0` and available-to-issue quantity `0`. | Reorder point `(3/7) × 7 + 0 = 3`; suggested quantity `3`. Using displayed `0.43` would incorrectly yield `ceil(3.01) = 4` (BR-009–BR-011, BR-015, BR-030). |
| Alert at threshold | Reorder point `8`; available-to-issue `8`; pending delivery `5`. | Show reorder alert and suggested quantity `0` separately; pending delivery does not change the calculation (BR-012/BR-013). |
| Quarantine and reorder input | On-hand `10`, of which `4` are quarantined; reorder point `8`; assume no other holds. | Available-to-issue quantity `6`; suggested quantity `2` (BR-030). Quarantine/disposal movements contribute zero forecast demand (BR-031). |
| FEFO and eligibility | Two eligible batches expire on different dates; a third batch is expired and quarantined. | Ordinary issue allocates from the earlier eligible expiry first; expired units cannot satisfy it even if transition evidence is delayed (BR-029/BR-033–BR-035; G-02). |
| Equal-expiry tie | Eligible B-20 and B-10 both expire 12 November; committed receiving sequences are 41 and 42 respectively. | Issue 1 unit from B-20 first despite its lexically later ID. If sequence also ties, stable batch ID decides under a Design-specified comparison convention (G-01 approved Option A). |
| FEFO before undated FIFO | Eligible dated D-1 and undated U-2/U-1 have committed receiving sequences 7/8 for U-2/U-1. Issue spans all three. | Consume D-1 first, then U-2, then U-1. Dated stock precedes all undated stock regardless of receipt order (BR-033/BR-034; G-01). |
| Singapore midnight expiry | One batch has 5 physical units and expires 8 November 2026; compare 7 November 23:59:59 with 8 November 00:00 Asia/Singapore. | Eligible before midnight; at 00:00 ordinary issue is denied, physical remains 5, available becomes 0, displayed quarantine becomes 5 even before stored transition (BR-029/BR-030/BR-035; G-02). |
| Delayed transition evidence | That batch crosses the boundary at 00:00; its evidence is recorded at 00:05. | Issue at 00:01 is denied; one system-attributed event records effective 00:00 and actual 00:05, with no duplicate or demand. If an authorised disposal of 2 occurs at 00:02, physical/quarantine are 3/3 and late evidence reconciles that movement. **Maximum permitted delay TBD** (G-02). |
| Atomic CSV positive-row rejection | In one `sku,change` upload for an explicitly selected authorized inventory, a valid negative row is followed by a positive row for an expiry-tracked product. | Reject the entire file with no stock mutation or demand record; receive the expiry-tracked product manually with batch/expiry details. Header stays `sku,change` (BR-004/BR-036). |
| Repeated positive CSV SKU | Non-expiry-tracked P receives `P,+5` then `P,+3` in one valid selected-inventory file; a subsequent `P,-6` issue occurs. | Two separate eligible undated batches are committed in row order with stable identities/sequences. The issue allocates 5 from the first, 1 from the second, leaving 2 physical units (G-03 approved Option A). |
| Positive then negative versus negative then positive | P starts with no eligible stock. Compare one file `P,+5` then `P,-2` with another file `P,-2` then `P,+5`. | First file commits with 3 units and linked receipt/issue evidence; second rejects entirely because the first row cannot borrow from a later receipt. No partial batch, stock, demand, marker or committed audit survives (G-03/G-04). |
| CSV byte and row boundaries | A syntactically valid file is 262,144 raw bytes with ≤1,000 data rows; compare 262,145 bytes with a valid row count. Separately compare 1,000 and 1,001 valid data rows within the byte limit. | 262,144 bytes and 1,000 rows pass their respective limit checks and may commit if all other rules pass. 262,145 bytes or 1,001 rows rejects wholly regardless of the other limit. These are planned tests, not capacity measurements (G-04). |
| Strict CSV syntax | Compare exact `sku,change` UTF-8/LF or CRLF with a BOM, malformed UTF-8, spaced header, blank record, extra field, `P,0`, `P,-0`, `P,1.5`, `P,1e3` or `P, 5`. | Only files meeting the approved strict contract can proceed to stock validation. Each invalid variant rejects the whole file with at least first failing row/field when applicable or a file-level category (G-04). |
| Concurrent identical CSV | Two authorised Managers concurrently submit the same valid raw bytes to the same selected inventory. | At most one import commits. The other leaves no stock, batch, demand, success marker or committed inventory-mutation audit changes; conflict response and locking mechanism remain Design TBD (BR-041/NFR-003/G-11). |
| Atomic CSV audit rollback | A valid negative row is followed by an invalid row in one selected-inventory CSV. | No stock, allocation, demand or committed inventory audit event from either row survives; any separate rejected-attempt log remains undecided (G-09 approved Option A). |
| Multi-batch stock-out audit | Staff issues 3 units, 2 from B-1 and 1 from B-2; physical/available quantity starts at 8. | One linked logical issue event identifies Staff, business/inventory/product, both allocations, quantity 8→5/change −3 and ordinary-demand classification. Demand is 3 units once, not two demand events (G-09 approved Option A). |
| Quarantined disposal | Manager submits disposal of `2` quarantined units with a reason; Staff submits the same action or Manager omits the reason. | Only the reasoned Manager action may commit, with actor/history retained; Staff and missing-reason actions are denied without mutation. Committed disposal contributes no demand (BR-031/BR-037). |
| Permanently reserved SKU | Product A in business X used SKU `ABC` and was deactivated. Manager attempts to give `abc` to new product B in X. | Reject reuse for B even though A is inactive; A's original SKU remains reserved (BR-001/BR-017/BR-038). Rename/alias behavior for A remains TBD. |
| False zero adjustment | P has physically present units but a Manager enters count 0 solely for deactivation. | Prohibited: a genuine absolute count adjustment requires actual-count evidence and mandatory reason; it cannot dispose of physical goods. Damaged nonquarantined and unclassified returned goods remain blocking exceptions (G-05). |
| Concurrent receipt/deactivation | P has zero physical stock; receipt and deactivation compete. | If receipt commits first, deactivation fails; if deactivation commits first, later ordinary receipt fails. No inactive product accepts newly received ordinary stock (G-05/G-11). |
| Cross-inventory deactivation guard | Product P has 4 available units in A and 2 quarantined units in B. | Deactivation denied. A legitimate authorised issue of A's 4 and reasoned Manager disposal of B's 2 leaves zero physical stock; only issue is demand. The atomic zero recheck then permits deactivation subject to G-13 authority. G-05 Option A, 3 November. |
| CSV inventory scope | Manager selects authorized inventory A before uploading two valid `sku,change` rows; inventory B has the same catalogue products. | Both committed rows affect A only; B remains unchanged. An unselected or unauthorized inventory cannot be used (BR-039). |
| Inventory-scoped byte duplicate | File bytes `F` imported successfully for inventory A. Re-upload `F` to A; separately upload `F` to authorized inventory B, assuming rows otherwise valid. | Reject re-upload to A with no mutation. B's upload is not rejected **solely** as a duplicate of A's success. A byte-different file with equivalent records is not identical under BR-041 (BR-005/BR-041). Concurrent identical same-inventory uploads cannot both commit; mechanism/response TBD (G-11). |
| Login and wrong password | Active/unknown/inactive identity attempts receive generic public status/timing. Independent limits: 5 failed logins/identity/15 minutes and 20/IP/15 minutes; sixth or twenty-first attempt in the relevant window is throttled. | **APPROVED BR-042 and G-07 Option A, 3 November 2026.** No extra lockout/ban duration approved; keys, windows and concurrent enforcement are Design. |
| Staff invitation | Authorised Manager invites Staff within current BR-027 scope; link is single-use/24-hour, only latest resend remains usable; expired, revoked, used or superseded link cannot create access. | **APPROVED BR-043 and G-07 Option A, 3 November 2026.** Email delivery and token implementation open. |
| Password reset token | Request R1 then R2: R1 fails; R2 succeeds once within 30 minutes; reuse/expiry fails; successful reset revokes prior sessions and requires sign-in. | **APPROVED BR-044 and G-07 Option A, 3 November 2026.** No executed test claimed. |
| Logout and deactivated session | Logout invalidates current session; deactivated Staff old session is denied on next protected request; owner-controlled suspected-compromise response revokes all affected sessions and requires reset while retaining attribution. | **APPROVED BR-026/BR-045 and G-07 Option A, 3 November 2026.** Exact invalidation mechanism Design TBD. |
| CSRF and inventory boundary | Authenticated user submits protected state-changing request without valid CSRF proof, then requests an unassigned or other-business inventory. | Reject invalid-CSRF mutation; authenticated session does not bypass assigned-inventory/business restriction (BR-022/BR-045). These cases require dated stock movement evidence and a trustworthy indication that each day was observed. The observation mechanism and database schema belong to later decisions.

1. Stock-out exactly equal to available stock; one unit over available stock; repeated concurrent stock-outs.
2. Adjustment below zero, adjustment to zero, and adjustment with missing reason if reasons are approved.
3. CSV valid rows around an invalid row; unknown SKU; repeated SKU; repeated whole file; malformed quotes; empty/header-only file; UTF-8 BOM; maximum size boundary.
4. Forecast all-zero fully observed week; one missing day; no history; demand transaction at day boundary; adjustment in window; lead time zero/one; reorder quantity fractional before ceiling.
5. Product SKU case/space variations and attempts to rename/deactivate a product with movements.

No example is an executed test. The individually approved rules are recorded separately; remaining details require owner disposition before Requirements exit.
