## Owner-approved first receipt rules — 5 November 2026

For an authenticated active Manager or Staff member, accept manual receipt only for an inventory in the same business with a current active assignment and a product active and linked to that inventory. Recheck and lock authorization in the write transaction. Assignment revocation committed first denies the receipt; if the receipt obtains its required authorization locks first, it may complete before revocation. Accept positive whole-number quantities in the product's labeled base unit; packaging conversion is deferred. Require expiry configuration before a product's first receipt. When mandatory expiry tracking is enabled, a valid expiry date is required. When it is not enabled, a manual receipt may omit expiry; if supplied, reject a date that is today or earlier in Asia/Singapore and apply expiry eligibility and dated FEFO. An undated receipt remains in the approved undated ordering. Existing products remain active. Implement product lifecycle and configuration fields `is_active` and `expiry_tracking_required`; no ordinary receipt is allowed for an inactive product.

Each receipt is at most 2,147,483,647 units, and the product/inventory physical balance may not exceed 2,147,483,647 units, counting quarantined stock. Reject any overflow atomically. A receiving form has a unique request token: same token plus identical receipt input returns the original receipt; same token with changed input is rejected; a fresh form/token permits a separate receipt. Recheck authorization even when returning the original result. Create one stable batch identity, committed receiving sequence, and exactly one attributable receipt audit event in the same PostgreSQL transaction. Any validation, authorization, batch, sequence, balance or audit failure leaves no receipt mutation committed.

These are owner-approved observable requirements and do not approve the complete SRS or Design entry. The paragraph records fields that were absent from TORY-13 during TORY-15 preparation; TORY-16 now implements the approved Product fields and receiving records. The base-unit label's 40-character column is a provisional storage bound, while configuration UX/lifecycle, broader stock movements and audit history remain outside this increment. InventoryProduct still represents the link and has no independent active flag.

## TORY-16 PostgreSQL transaction and locking protocol — implementation evidence, not a new owner policy

TORY-16 uses the business-owned **Product row as the shared anchor** for receipt operations for that product. This is an implemented technical choice subject to review when future stock writers and deactivation are designed:

1. Authenticate, enforce CSRF, validate basic form syntax and perform an initial authorization check before taking locks. Do not reveal product/inventory details to an unauthorized caller.
2. Start `transaction.atomic()` and lock User, active BusinessMembership and active InventoryAssignment rows `FOR SHARE`. Revocation updates conflict with these locks; concurrent receipts can share them.
3. Lock Product `FOR UPDATE`, then Inventory `FOR UPDATE`, then InventoryProduct `FOR UPDATE`. Product serializes same-product receipts across inventories; the association row serializes each inventory balance and sequence. The locked rows are rechecked before mutation.
4. Migration `catalogue/0004_product_first_receipt_lock_order.py` changes the existing InventoryProduct parent trigger from Inventory→Product to Product→Inventory, and `receiving/0003_receipt_integrity_and_audit_guards.py` follows the same parent order. Because an UPDATE locks the InventoryProduct row before its BEFORE trigger, `receiving/0004_preserve_received_inventory_product_identity.py` rejects association identity reassignment rather than introducing a link-row→parent lock cycle. The assignment trigger's membership→inventory order is compatible with the receipt's earlier membership/assignment authorization locks.
5. Read one authoritative server time after lock acquisition, convert it to Asia/Singapore, and reject expiry dates on or before that local date. Asia/Singapore date semantics are approved; choosing PostgreSQL or another trusted clock source and handling clock failure remain technical review items.
6. The database trigger requires the next sequence from InventoryProduct's committed counter and advances that counter with the physical balance when the batch is inserted. A unique `(inventory_product, receipt_sequence)` constraint remains a second guard. CSV is not implemented; any future receipt source must deliberately join this ordering protocol.
7. Batch, actor-scoped request token, quantity update and exactly one receipt audit row commit atomically. The SHA-256 fingerprint uses normalized inventory/product IDs, integer quantity and ISO expiry date; database uniqueness is `(actor, source, token)`. A concurrent same-token collision either resolves as replay after locked authorization recheck or fails on changed input; a failed audit rolls the token and receipt back. `receiving/0005` checks audit after-quantity against the committed-in-transaction InventoryProduct balance. No automatic deadlock retry is implemented; a user retry uses the same form token and reauthorizes.

The Product lock protects only operations that acquire it and hold it through commit. Future ordinary stock-out and deactivation paths must use a reviewed compatible anchor if they change/check physical stock. TORY-16 also locks InventoryProduct and database triggers prevent direct counter updates; batch/audit triggers enforce same-business scope, aggregate limits, next sequence and committed audit presence. These controls do not implement future allocation, expiry transitions or deactivation. Sequence increments roll back with failed receipts, so committed sequence order has no gaps caused by failed TORY-16 receipts.

## Open decisions and bounded deferrals

### Remaining review and later decisions

- The approved quantity limits, base-unit label, active state and explicit expiry configuration are implemented; existing products migrate active and unconfigured. No default tracking policy is inferred.
- Review the implemented actor-scoped token/fingerprint and PostgreSQL locks as part of increment review; two-connection tests cover duplicate token, aggregate overflow, sequence order and both assignment/revocation orderings.
- Product-first trigger reconciliation and committed receipt audit rollback are implemented in migrations/tests. Future stock writers must be reviewed against this lock order; the Product lock is not blanket approval of a future deactivation protocol.
- TORY-16 stores the label, active/configuration values, batch, balance, sequence and one attributable event. It does not decide product configuration change lifecycle after receiving history exists; the operator command only initializes unconfigured products.

### Later blockers and proposed bounded deferrals — NOT APPROVED

- **G-02:** maximum delay for expiry-boundary evidence remains blocking. TORY-16 may record expiry dates and the receipt event, but a proposed bound is to decide the delay before implementing expiry quarantine transitions in the expiry/history increment (TORY-20 or its predecessor). This does not defer the approved Singapore-midnight availability rule or claim G-02 closed.
- **G-05:** damaged non-quarantined and unclassified returned physical stock remain unresolved; deactivation must remain unavailable while such units are present. The mandatory disposal reason's exact format also remains open. Resolve before implementing deactivation/disposal; no generic write-off is proposed. Do not use a fake product flag to claim deactivation is complete.
- **G-09:** keep the approved committed receipt event in TORY-16 and ensure rejected requests create no committed inventory-mutation event. A bounded proposal is to decide separate rejected-attempt security/error logging before the security/audit acceptance gate or deployment; owner acceptance is required, and G-09 remains blocking until then.
- **TORY-17:** implement and verify FEFO/FIFO allocation, equal-expiry sequence/ID ties, dated-before-undated ordering, shortages, nonnegative stock, and competing stock-out races. G-01 ordering is approved; no allocation implementation belongs in TORY-16.
- **TORY-20 and other stock work:** implement authorized balance/history views, durable movement/audit history, expiry transition evidence and any disposal/deactivation workflow at their own scope. Preserve the G-02 delay and G-05 physical-stock blockers. Other open CSV, forecast and G-11 concurrency cases remain with their dependent increments.
