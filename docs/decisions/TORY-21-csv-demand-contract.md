## Existing approved CSV contract — TORY-22

- An assigned Manager selects one authorized, same-business inventory before upload. Every row applies there. Staff import is denied. The exact file is `sku,change`: strict UTF-8 without BOM; exact lowercase header; LF or CRLF; exactly two CSV fields per data record with standard quoting for commas/quotes; no blank records or embedded record newlines. Trim outer SKU spaces for lookup, but do not trim `change`. Require nonzero base-10 whole-number text matching `[+-]?[0-9]+`. Empty or header-only files fail.
- The inclusive limits are **262,144 raw bytes** (including header and line endings) and **1,000 data rows** (excluding header). Each positive row is also subject to the separately approved 2,147,483,647-unit receipt and product/inventory physical-balance cap, including quarantine. These are acceptance limits, not measured capacity.
- Each valid positive row for a non-expiry-tracked product creates its own eligible undated batch with stable identity and committed receipt sequence. Repeated SKUs run sequentially in file order without aggregation. Manual and CSV undated receipts share approved receipt ordering; eligible dated batches precede undated batches. Positive rows for expiry-tracked products remain prohibited. Negative rows issue eligible stock using approved FEFO/FIFO and the state after preceding rows; a later positive row cannot rescue an earlier insufficient issue.
- The entire selected-inventory import commits or rolls back together, including stock, batches, demand, success marker and linked committed audit evidence. A prior successful import of identical raw bytes into the same inventory is rejected; failed imports, success in another inventory and byte-different semantic equivalents do not meet BR-041 duplicate identity. Rejection identifies at least the first failing row and field where applicable, or a file-level category. Concurrent identical imports cannot both commit.

## Existing approved demand observation contract — TORY-23

- Demand is committed ordinary manual and negative-CSV stock-out quantity, counted once per issue even across batches. A timestamp-free `sku,change` issue uses its successful import/commit timestamp and the corresponding Asia/Singapore calendar day. The CSV contract has no occurrence-date field and cannot backdate historical demand. Receipts, physical-count adjustments, expiry quarantine and Manager disposal are non-demand; a count adjustment cannot stand in for a missing historical issue.
- For each product, inventory and **complete** Asia/Singapore calendar day, an assigned same-business Manager verifies attributable observation only after that day ends. Staff cannot verify. A verified no-issue day contributes zero; absence of movements alone does not establish coverage. The forecast uses the previous seven complete Singapore days, excluding the current day. Any missing, incomplete or invalidated day in that window makes the numeric forecast and dependent reorder recommendation unavailable. Calculate with full precision and display the average to two decimals; downstream calculations use the unrounded value.
- A later discovery of a missing issue invalidates the affected day's verification and dependent output until an approved historical correction and Manager reverification. Verification cannot be borrowed from another product or inventory.

## Owner-approved bounded CA1 MVP deferral — 8 November 2026

An **assigned Manager** may mark a **previously verified product, inventory and Singapore calendar day** incomplete and record the reason. If an approved historical correction is unavailable, that day remains incomplete. **While it falls within the previous seven complete Singapore calendar days, suppress the numeric forecast and dependent reorder recommendation.** A corrected day requires Manager reverification. **In-app historical correction is deferred for the CA1 MVP.**

This is the operational path for reporting a newly discovered gap without inventing a correction transaction or treating an adjustment as demand. It preserves G-06's verified-zero, missing-day and after-day-end rules. It does not alter the CSV timestamp contract or permit historical backdating. Reason field format, evidence retention, the procedure and authority for any external approved correction, and concurrent invalidation/reverification mechanics remain Design questions. If no approved correction exists, the day cannot be reverified as complete merely to restore a numeric result.

## TORY-23 owner decisions — 9 November 2026

- Manual issues use the existing action timestamp (`occurred_at`) for their Singapore demand day, including an action before midnight committed afterward. Negative CSV issues use the **actual PostgreSQL transaction commit time**, not their row audit time or TORY-22's pre-commit `StockImport.committed_at` sample.
- Coverage begins at each environment's activation. Earlier dates are not observed or backfilled; a partial Singapore date cannot be counted as a complete observed date. Seven complete post-activation dates still require explicit assigned-Manager verification.
- CSV stock imports continue when commit-time tracking is unavailable, but unresolved attribution or unavailable tracking suppresses numeric forecast and dependent recommendation output. No pre-commit timestamp substitutes for actual commit time.
- The bounded CA1 deferral above remains: no in-app historical correction or unsupported reverification. An invalidated date without approved external correction stays incomplete.

**TORY-23 implementation and verification boundary:** `StockImport.committed_at` retains its existing pre-commit meaning. New imports store a transaction ID and finalize a separate actual commit timestamp after commit, with a retryable recovery command; pending and unavailable attribution never becomes numeric demand. Coverage activation uses a future Singapore date and actual commit-time validation, with no historical backfill. The local PostgreSQL database now reports `track_commit_timestamp=on`; the owner reports 133 local PostgreSQL tests passing without skips on `b2e4222`. [Django PostgreSQL CI](https://github.com/Mike-Franco/ay2627s2-cicdp-MikeAbat/actions/runs/42554980980) and [Documentation CI](https://github.com/Mike-Franco/ay2627s2-cicdp-MikeAbat/actions/runs/42554980773) passed for that commit. Tests cover real PostgreSQL commit-time capture and recovery; a subsequent local PostgreSQL test checks attribution with fixed timestamps on both sides of Singapore midnight. No test has held a real transaction across 00:00 Asia/Singapore. Reason validation uses nonblank trimmed text of at most 2,000 characters as a Design choice consistent with existing movement reasons. External correction authority/evidence and later reverification remain unresolved.

## Open Design and verification work

- **TORY-22:** parser and raw-byte identity storage/retention, import and row audit linkage, committed sequence under concurrent manual/CSV receipts, whole-file locking/rollback, G-11 races, G-12 numeric representation, G-13 assignment-change timing, G-16 SKU characters, G-19 broader feedback, and measured file-limit performance remain open. The approved syntax, bounds and duplicate identity must not change to resolve them.
- **TORY-23:** daily coverage and reason records, timestamp attribution, activation evidence, late-discovery invalidation, concurrent verification/invalidation, and unavailable-state presentation are implemented on the feature branch. A real transaction crossing Singapore midnight, operational finalization monitoring, evidence-retention policy, and integration with a future numeric reorder recommendation remain open. The external approved-correction process is unresolved; the CA1 MVP can leave the day incomplete. No historical-correction UI is authorized by this note.
- **Separate TORY-19 verification:** the approved one-hour expiry-quarantine evidence limit still needs scheduler and staging proof. It is not a TORY-21 CSV or demand-coverage acceptance result.
