# Tory Planning Risk Register

**Date:** 29 October 2026  
**Confirmed constraint context:** Deadline 27 November 2026; one solo developer; 10–15 project hours/week; cash ceiling SGD 0 (zero-cost services only).  
**Owner convention:** “Solo developer” owns technical and delivery risks; “Project owner” owns scope/approval and budget decisions. Named individuals and escalation arrangements beyond these roles are TBD.

## Scoring scale

- **Likelihood (L):** 1 Rare, 2 Unlikely, 3 Possible, 4 Likely, 5 Almost certain.
- **Impact (I):** 1 Negligible, 2 Minor, 3 Moderate, 4 Major, 5 Critical to stage acceptance or project viability.
- **Risk score:** `L × I`, range 1–25. Bands: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Moderate, 10–16 High, 17–25 Very high. Scores are initial Planning estimates, not measured probabilities; review at each stage gate.

The owner must review priorities, owners, response authority and residual risk; no mitigation authorizes paid use or scope reduction. Review at each stage gate and weekly while the deadline is near.
